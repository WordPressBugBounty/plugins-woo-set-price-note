Clarify your pricing. Boost customer confidence and reduce support queries by adding custom units, offer details, or edition labels directly to your product prices.

== Description ==

Confused customers don't buy. If your products are sold in specific units (e.g., "per kg," "per pack") or if you have seasonal offers that need highlighting, **Set Price Note for WooCommerce** is the perfect solution.

This plugin empowers shop managers to append custom text, units, or messages directly to the product price across your entire store—from the shop archive all the way through to the final order confirmation email. By providing clear pricing context, you eliminate ambiguity and improve the overall shopping experience.

### Why Stores Use Set Price Note
* **Instant Clarification:** Clearly display units (e.g., "$10 / 500g") to avoid customer confusion.
* **Drive Sales with Offers:** Highlight limited-time deals or holiday offers directly next to the price.
* **Enhance Professionalism:** Add edition types or custom notes to make your store look more polished and informative.
* **Consistency Everywhere:** Unlike basic text plugins, this displays your note in the Cart, Checkout, and even your Transactional Emails, ensuring the customer is never in doubt.
* **Fully Flexible:** Works with any product type—Simple, Variable, or otherwise.

== Features ==

* **Total Price Context:** Add custom labels to the price display on shop pages, single products, and archives.
* **Checkout & Email Integration:** Optional settings to include your price notes in Cart views, Checkout pages, Order Details, and Customer Emails.
* **Custom Formatting:** Control the separator style (e.g., " - ", " | ", "/") between the price and your custom note.
* **Control Panel:** Dedicated settings tab for managing how notes appear in transactional documents.
* **Label Customization:** Fully customize the display labels for order details and email notifications to match your brand.
* **Easy Implementation:** No coding required. Simply add your text in the product edit screen and save.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the `woo-set-price-note` folder to the `/wp-content/plugins/` directory.
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' menu in WordPress.
3. Open any product edit screen in WooCommerce.
4. Locate the new "Price Note" fields within the Product Data section.
5. Enter your unit, offer, or note, choose your separator, and update the product.
6. Check your front-end store to see your price note in action!

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does this work with variable products? =
Yes! It supports all standard WooCommerce product types.

= Can I choose where the note appears? =
Yes, you can enable or disable the note display for the Cart, Checkout page, Order Details, and Customer Emails through the plugin's settings.

= Is it easy to change the text later? =
Absolutely. Because the setting is saved per product, you can update your offer messages or units at any time directly from the product edit page.

= Does this affect my SEO? =
No, the plugin simply adds a visual note to the price display and does not interfere with the product schema or SEO structure.
